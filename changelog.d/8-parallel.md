# 8-parallel: parallel items (OPT-IN, evidence-gated): scheduler, leases, schema mutex, ports, worktrees, serial integration, reject cascade

## Changes

- `.claude/scripts/loopctl/parallel/` (Python 3.9, standard library). `parallel_mode: worktree` makes `driver.run` build a `scheduler.ParallelDriver` instead of a `Driver`; with `parallel_mode: off` (the default) the package is never imported. Spec: `.claude/spec/loopctl-parallel.md`; operator guide: `docs/parallel.md`.
  - `scheduler.py`: `ParallelDriver` **is** the 4c driver (same lock holder, `setup`, recovery, checkpoints, budget parking and Phase 7 hooks) with a different `run_items`. The main thread admits items, harvests ended workers, integrates, and invalidates; one `Worker` thread per item runs the serial driver's stages in the item's own worktree. A `Worker` is a `Driver` that borrows everything shared from the parent (`__getattr__`) and owns only what is per item (its worktree as `repo`, a `Context` whose repo is that worktree, its own `Loop`, an environment with its port block); it never merges, switches the loop worktree or renames a branch.
  - Admission: dependencies **merged on the integration branch**, no footprint conflict with any item holding a worktree or waiting for a human, no skipped earlier item it would overtake, lane mutexes free, a port slot free, fewer than `max_parallel` worktrees, `Loop.begin(max_active=max_parallel)`. Draining on a park, a stop, a crash or a checkpoint (finish the child in hand, integrate what is finished, start nothing).
  - `footprint.py`: conservative glob overlap (`may_overlap`; "when it cannot prove two globs apart it says they meet"), `conflict(a, b)` (overlap, an item with no `touches.paths` runs alone, the **schema** lane for migrations/`touches.schema` and the **deps** lane for `touches.deps_change`), and the check of an item's real diff against its promise (`undeclared`).
  - `mutex.py`: leases in `<git-common-dir>/loopctl/leases/` (`item-<id>`, `mutex-schema`, `mutex-deps`, `ports-<n>`, `integrate`); a secret **token** proves ownership, every read-modify-write is under an `flock`; reclaimable when the owner pid is certainly dead on this host, or when the heartbeat is older than `lease_ttl_s` and `alive(record)` says the owner is gone; unreadable counts as held; `acquire_mutexes` is all-or-nothing in one order.
  - `ports.py`: a slot per worker shifts every `verify_ports` entry by `slot * 100`, skipped when its lease is held or any of its ports is in use (`verify.port_busy`); the shift travels as `KIT_PORT_OFFSET` (plus `KIT_PORT_SLOT`, `KIT_PORTS`, `KIT_ITEM`). `verify.verify(port_offset=)` and G6 carry it.
  - `worktrees.py`: the only place a worktree is created or removed: `<repo>/.claude/worktrees/<slug>-a<n>` from `git worktree add <path> <existing branch>`, path-safe (plain name, no symlink on the way, strictly under the root), excluded from the clone's `status`; `.worktreeinclude` (only `.env.local` and `.env.test`, 0600, no symlinks); `cleanup_orphans` saves a dead worker's uncommitted work on the item's branch before it removes the worktree.
  - `integrate.py`: the only writer of the integration branch: leases checked, **footprint** check (a lie parks `footprint_violation`), **rebase** onto a moved tip (a conflict is aborted and parks `merge_conflict`), **migration order** (`item/migration_order` sends the item back to its engineer), **re-verify** (`g6` and the merge transition's gates, in the item's worktree), the lease check, `Driver.merge_branch`. A lease lost before the merge changes nothing (`lost`; the fourth loss parks `lease_lost`).
  - `cascade.py`: the reject cascade (L-6): merged dependents reverted newest first on the local integration branch with their non-`lossy` down-migrations (`abort.run_down`), everything touched `pending` with a fresh attempt, in-flight descendants invalidated (cancelled at a safe point, work kept on `dead/*`); a human revert plan instead of any action once an item has reached `main` or delivery is not `local`; it cannot loop (visited-set closure, never self-triggering, `MAX_CASCADES` per root).
- `loopctl parallel check|status|leases|cleanup|cascade` (`cleanup` and `cascade` do nothing without `--apply`, and refuse while a run holds the lock). Classified in `cli.py` (`FLAG_READ["parallel"]`, tier 1; `cascade --apply` also needs Tier 2) and `guards/g_exec.py`; 24 corpus rows in `tests/guards/corpus/loopctl.json`.
- `.claude/scripts/worktree_hook.py create|remove`: the WorktreeCreate / WorktreeRemove hook (D-3, D-22). It cuts the worktree off the **local** integration tip, under `.claude/worktrees/<name>` only, copies `.worktreeinclude`, reserves a port slot, prints the path; any name that is not one plain name, any path that does not resolve inside the root, a symlink on the way, or an input shape it does not know exits 2. Snippet `.claude/templates/parallel/worktree-hooks.settings.json` (not wired by the kit) and `.claude/templates/worktreeinclude`.
- `.claude/spec/pipeline-host-workflow.md`: `pipeline_host: workflow`, DESIGN-ONLY. Doctor checks `parallel.pipeline-host` (error on `workflow`, evidence or not) and `parallel.mode` (`doctorlib/parallel.py`); `loopctl run`'s preflight prints the `parallel.*` rows.
- Seams in the 4a-4c code (all behaviour-neutral for a serial run): `Driver.child_allowance / child_ended / count_child / recover_merge / merge_branch` and `only_item`; `Loop.begin(max_active=)`; `locked.refresh` counts every item in progress; `abort.run_down`; `unattended.json` gains `items`; `run.json` gains `currentItems`.
- Tests: `tests/loopctl/parallel/` (113 tests, about 15 CPU-seconds in all; the slowest module, six runs end to end, is about 5 s alone): footprints, leases, ports, worktrees, the hook, integration, the cascade, the scheduler's decisions, the CLI, config/doctor/classification, and six runs end to end with a per-item fake `claude` (`par_claude.py`, a front end to the shared fake).

## Upgrade impact

None for anyone who does not opt in: `parallel_mode` defaults to `off`, the package is not imported, and the serial driver's behaviour is unchanged (the seams answer as before). A v1.2.0-to-v2 install gains four registered keys (below), the `loopctl parallel` command, the `parallel.*` doctor rows (skipped while `parallel_mode` is off) and `worktree_hook.py` (not wired). Anyone who sets `parallel_mode: worktree` also needs `runner: driver`, the evidence gate and a plan whose items declare `touches.paths`; otherwise it runs one item at a time.

## New config keys

All owner `8-parallel`, section "Parallel items".

- `parallel_mode` (enum `off|worktree`, `off`, H6, evidence-gated for `worktree`).
- `max_parallel` (int, 2, H6): clamped to 1..4 in code and checked by the doctor.
- `pipeline_host` (enum `conductor|workflow`, `conductor`, H6, evidence-gated for `workflow`): `workflow` is a doctor error and a preflight refusal.
- `lease_ttl_s` (int, 300, not H6): the heartbeat age after which a lease whose owner is also gone can be reclaimed.

## Decisions made

- **One scheduler, not a second driver.** `ParallelDriver` subclasses `Driver` and a `Worker` is a `Driver` that delegates the shared parts to its parent. The alternative (a separate scheduler calling the driver's hooks) would have copied the 4c stage code or forced every stage to take a repo path. Consequence: the Phase 7 supervisor, the checkpoints, the crash recovery and the unattended hooks work unchanged, and `Supervisor.attach` is still their only user.
- **Threads in the driver's process.** Workers are threads, so the lock holder, `driverPid`, the ledger, the session registry and the `children/` records are the serial driver's. Child sessions are still their own process groups. Git is safe across worktrees; every write to the integration branch is on the main thread.
- **`pipeline_host` choices are `conductor|workflow`**, the report's names (11.2), not the brief's example `local|workflow`; orchestrate.md already says `conductor`.
- **Leases.** The report names leases without defining them, so: one claim per file under the common dir, a random token, `flock` around every read-modify-write, reclaim when the owner pid is certainly gone or when expired and not alive. A hook-created port slot has pid 0 and a 7-day TTL (the hook process ends at once); the scheduler's start ignores those.
- **Mutexes are held until merge.** The schema (and deps) lane is held from an item's start until it has merged, parked, or been put back, so two migrations never sit in two branches and "regenerate timestamped migrations" reduces to a check (`item/migration_order`) for the case where someone committed a migration by hand. Items waiting for a human keep their footprint and lanes in the computed rule but give their leases back.
- **An item that declares no `touches.paths` runs alone**, and a candidate may not overtake an earlier skipped one it conflicts with (no starvation).
- **Budget reservation beyond DEC-34's division.** `remaining / workers` can still overspend when children launch at different moments (the first sees one worker and takes everything), so each child is also capped by what no other live child was promised, and a worker waits when that is nothing. Two-phase start (`pending_spawn`) so a round's workers are counted together.
- **Peers are not killed.** HALT, a lost lock and a signal end running children (as in the serial driver). A park, a budget stop or a cascade lets the child in hand finish and stops the worker at the next safe point: killing it would record an unknown cost and park the run (IMP-25).
- **Approved items are merged three-way, unrebased** (`merge_approved` is the serial path): the human approved a head, and a rebase would change it. The gap (no re-verify after other items merged) is recorded as not built.
- **A drain, not a stop, for one item's park.** The others finish and finished items are merged; the report's "park and stop" is kept at run level (exit 1).
- **No install/ephemeral-DB step.** Stack-specific; the slot (`KIT_PORT_OFFSET`, `KIT_ITEM`) is the key a project's script uses.
- **The hook is a snippet, not a manifest entry**: `hooks.manifest.json` has no opt-in entries and generates the headless child profiles; a child must not create worktrees. A hook-created worktree cannot have a `<slug>-a<n>` name (the driver's orphan cleanup would take it for a dead worker's), and a hook cannot remove a driver's worktree.
- **`locked_paths.json` is one file for every session**, so with several items active an engineer is not kept out of another active item's lane by it; each worker has its own worktree and the integrator refuses a diff outside the declared footprint.

Adversarial attempts, each with a test (`tests/loopctl/parallel/`):

- two workers claiming one item (eight threads racing for one lease: exactly one wins; the loser of `start_item` leaves no lease and no state change; a lane or port slot taken gives back what was already taken);
- a footprint lie (an undeclared file, a file moved out of the footprint, a deleted file outside it; `DOCS/` case variants are lies; the item's own custody trail is not; hostile globs `..`, absolute, `**` alone are dropped, an item with none runs alone);
- a port collision (a stranger listening on a slot's port moves the worker to the next slot; every slot taken or busy means no start; a shift past 65535 ends the slots; **found and fixed**: `KIT_PORT_OFFSET` accepted other scripts' digits through `str.isdigit()`, now ASCII digits only);
- a lease that expires mid-merge (a rival that thinks our owner is dead takes the integration slot in the `before_merge` seam: nothing merged, the item stays `reviewing`; losing the item's own lease three times then parks it);
- a cascade that loops (cyclic `dependsOn`; a root cascaded four times; an item invalidated more than three times; a worktree that will not go);
- a WorktreeCreate path outside `.claude/worktrees` (`../../escape`, absolute, separators, spaces, 80 characters, `.lock`, a `<slug>-a<n>` look-alike, a non-string, a symlinked `.claude` or `.claude/worktrees`, a `remove` aimed at the repository, `.git`, `/`, a symlink to another directory, a path with `..`);
- **found and fixed on the way**: `Driver.merge_branch` fell back to the whole `recover()` when the merge record failed, which in a parallel run would have reset every other worker's item and removed their worktrees (now `recover_merge`); a rejected root that was parked by the cascade's own plan-only step was reset to `pending` straight afterwards; a ready slot whose item had moved under it would have been offered to the integrator for ever; a hook-made worktree named like a driver's would have been swept as an orphan.
